\begin{abstract}
This document lists exactly one hundred sharply stated conjectures in geometric analysis: fifty lesser-known targets and fifty problems of medium fame.  The most famous flagship problems are deliberately omitted.  Every item includes a primary or authoritative source, a status note, a concrete line of attack, and a deliberately coarse estimate of the chance of obtaining either a proof or a counterexample.  The estimates are research triage, not calibrated mathematical probabilities.
\end{abstract}

\section*{How to read the list}

The score $\psettle$ is the estimated chance that a focused project---roughly one strong investigator working for several weeks to a few months, with symbolic computation, interval arithmetic, or numerical reconnaissance where appropriate---would obtain a publishable proof or disproof of the \emph{literal statement}.  It is not the probability that the statement is true.  Universal statements with an exposed finite-dimensional ansatz can therefore score highly even when they look deep.  Every displayed score is at least $50\%$ by construction.

``Lesser-known'' means that the conjecture is recent, buried in a remark, computationally motivated, or confined to a small specialty.  ``Medium fame'' means that the problem recurs in surveys or has a recognizable name, but is not one of the field's dominant mega-conjectures.  Closely related entries are retained only when their conclusions are genuinely distinct; shared machinery is noted.  Status was checked against arXiv and later primary literature through the date above, but an absence-of-resolution claim should still be rechecked before beginning a project.

\section{Fifty lesser-known conjectures}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{L\arabic*.}]

\cand{Xu's zero-mode scalar-curvature sign conjecture (counterexample found)}
{Let $(M^n,g)$ be compact with smooth nonempty boundary, $n\ge3$, and let the first Robin eigenvalue of the conformal Laplacian be $\eta'_1=0$.  Xu conjectured that whenever a conformal metric realizes a nonzero prescribed boundary mean curvature $H$, its prescribed scalar curvature $S$ must change sign and satisfy $\int_M S\,dV_g<0$.}
{J. Xu, Remark 5.1 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.15024}{arXiv:2211.15024v2} (2022/23).  The literal statement is false; no later correction was found in the targeted search.}
{On flat $[-1,1]\times T^{n-1}$ take $u=A-y^2$ and $\widetilde g=u^{4/(n-2)}g$.  Then $S=2a u^{1-p}>0$ and $H=-(p-2)(A-1)^{-p/2}<0$.  Appendix~\ref{app:xu} gives the complete check.}
{$\psettle=99\%$; false.  This is included as a proof-of-concept result produced during the search.}

\cand{Affine-maximal Bernstein threshold}
{If $f\in C^\infty(\Omega)$ is strictly convex on a convex $\Omega\subset\mathbb R^2$, satisfies $\sum f^{ij}w_{ij}=0$ with $w=(\det D^2f)^a$ and $a\notin[-2/3,-1/3]$, and the Calabi metric $D^2f$ is complete, then $f$ is quadratic.}
{Y. Sun and R. Xu, p.~3 of \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08525}{arXiv:2501.08525} (2025).  Open at the cutoff.}
{Reduce radial and cohomogeneity-one solutions to an autonomous ODE and exhaust complete asymptotics; if no counterexample survives, combine the Legendre transform with the two-dimensional maximum principle.}
{$\psettle=65\%$; lean true.}

\cand{CMC index versus area and genus}
{For every branched CMC-$h$ immersion $u:\Sigma^2\to N^3$ into a three-manifold of strictly positive sectional curvature, there is $C(N)>0$ such that
\[C(N)\bigl((1+h^2)\operatorname{Area}(\Sigma)+g(\Sigma)\bigr)\le \operatorname{ind}(A_h)+\operatorname{nul}(A_h).\]}
{L. Seemungal and B. Sharp, Conjecture 1.4 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02932}{arXiv:2411.02932} (2024).  Open.}
{Sharpen the Ejiri--Micallef energy comparison and test every constant against explicit CMC families in Berger spheres.  A collapsing Berger example is also a credible disproof route.}
{$\psettle=55\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Algebraic harmonic maps are totally symmetric}
{For a compact or noncompact Riemann surface $M$ and an inner semisimple symmetric space $G/K$ with $G$ connected and center-free, every algebraic harmonic map $M\to G/K$ is totally symmetric; equivalently its extended-frame monodromy is trivial.}
{J. Dorfmeister and P. Wang, \S5.2 of \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12487}{arXiv:2408.12487} (2024).  Open.}
{Write finite-Laurent DPW potentials on an annulus or cylinder and compute monodromy exactly.  The breadth of the noncompact and nonsimply-connected formulation makes a small-rank counterexample plausible.}
{$\psettle=80\%$; lean false.}

\cand{Gaussian boundary area of convex bodies is below two (counterexample found)}
{For every closed convex body $C\subset\mathbb R^{n+1}$ with nonempty interior,
\[\mathcal H(C)=(4\pi)^{-n/2}\int_{\partial C}e^{-|x|^2/4}\,d\mathcal H^n<2.\]}
{Conjecture I of Angenent--Daskalopoulos--Sesum, \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.17272}{arXiv:2305.17272} (2023).  The literal dimension-free statement is false.}
{For the cube $C=[-a,a]^d$, $d=n+1$, direct integration gives
\[\mathcal H(C)=2d\,e^{-a^2/4}\operatorname{erf}(a/2)^{d-1}.\]
Taking $d=1000$ and $a=5$ makes this quantity greater than two; elementary bounds on $e$ and $\operatorname{erfc}(5/2)$ turn the numerical observation into a short rigorous disproof.}
{$\psettle=99\%$; false.}

\cand{Magnetic Cheeger positivity}
{For compact $M$ with smooth boundary and magnetic potential $\eta$ not gauge-equivalent to zero, the two magnetic Cheeger constants of Definition 2.4 satisfy
\[h^\eta(M)(h^\eta)'(M)>0.\]}
{T. Chakradhar, K. Gittins, G. Habib and N. Peyerimhoff, after Theorem 2.5 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07462}{arXiv:2410.07462} (2024/25).  Open.}
{Use BV compactness for minimizing $S^1$-valued gauges.  A vanishing sequence should converge to a global gauge, contradicting the flux obstruction; thin-neck examples stress-test compactness.}
{$\psettle=70\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Large-domain magnetic frustration}
{If $\eta$ is not gauge-equivalent to zero, there exist $\varepsilon,\delta>0$ such that every smooth relatively compact $D\subset M$ with $|D|\ge(1-\varepsilon)|M|$ and $|\partial_I D|\le\varepsilon$ has frustration $\iota^\eta(D)\ge\delta$.}
{Technical conjecture on p.~12 of \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07462}{arXiv:2410.07462}.  Open; it is a proposed route to the preceding item.}
{Extend near-minimizing gauges across the small complement and take an $S^1$-valued BV limit.  Quantifying the extension cost is the finite technical core.}
{$\psettle=80\%$; lean true.}

\cand{All-order identity for umbilics of W-congruences}
{At a nondegenerate umbilic of a W-congruence, the exceptional Taylor equation $W_{m0}=0$ follows automatically from $W=0$ for every integer $m\ge1$.  The authors verify the identity through $m=4$.}
{M. Craizer and R. A. Garcia, equation (3.4) and p.~13 of \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07289}{arXiv:2504.07289} (2025).  Open.}
{Compute the differential ideal generated by the W-equation, identify the invariant recurrence, and turn symbolic evidence into an induction.}
{$\psettle=85\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Generic integer W-singularity}
{Fix an integer $m\ge1$ and a nondegenerate umbilic in the source's W-jet space.  For an open dense subset of the corresponding W-jets, its discriminant singularity is of type $A_m$.}
{\S5.1 of \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07289}{arXiv:2504.07289}; proved through $m=4$, with examples for all $m$.  Open in general.}
{Combine the preceding all-order identity with a jet-transversality calculation and an explicit description of the exceptional algebraic set.}
{$\psettle=70\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Generic noninteger W-singularity}
{Fix a noninteger parameter $m$ and a nondegenerate umbilic in the source's W-jet space.  For an open dense subset of the corresponding W-jets, its discriminant singularity is of type $A_\infty$.}
{\S5.1 of \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07289}{arXiv:2504.07289} (2025).  Open.}
{Use finite-determinacy theory to show that every finite $A_k$ condition is resonant and hence nongeneric; search symbolic jets for a noninteger resonance as a disproof test.}
{$\psettle=60\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Existence of an immersed CMC sphere for every $c$}
{For every Riemannian metric on $S^3$ and every $c>0$, there exists a CMC-$c$ immersion $S^2\to S^3$.}
{L. Mazurowski and X. Zhou, Conjecture 1.5 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08228}{arXiv:2511.08228} (2025).  A branched immersion is known for almost every $c$; the unbranched all-$c$ statement is open.}
{Close the exceptional parameter set by curvature/area compactness and continuation of the min--max family, or construct a metric with an excluded mean-curvature interval.}
{$\psettle=55\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Two immersed CMC spheres for every $c$}
{Under the preceding hypotheses there are at least two CMC-$c$ immersions $S^2\to S^3$.}
{Mazurowski--Zhou, Conjecture 1.6 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08228}{arXiv:2511.08228}.  Open.}
{Apply Lusternik--Schnirelmann theory to the immersion-space min--max construction; alternatively, seek a strongly asymmetric metric with a unique solution.}
{$\psettle=50\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Two generic prescribed-mean-curvature spheres}
{For generic $h:S^3\to\mathbb R$ on the round sphere, at least two immersed two-spheres have prescribed mean curvature $h$.}
{Mazurowski--Zhou, Conjecture 1.7 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08228}{arXiv:2511.08228}.  Open.}
{Compute the degree of the PMC moduli map near the round problem and combine it with a second min--max class; numerically test highly asymmetric $h$ for uniqueness.}
{$\psettle=52\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Multi-peak exit-set components in prescribed scalar curvature}
{On a closed negative-Yamabe manifold with $R_{g_0}=-1$ and $\ker L_{g_0}=0$, a sufficiently separated $m$-peak target admits, for each $1\le q<m$ and suitable background level $\bar\alpha=\bar\alpha(q)$, additional distinct exit-set components represented by $q$-bubble states centered near $q$ distinct peaks.}
{M. Mayer and C. Zhu, Remark 4.3(ii) in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10639}{arXiv:2406.10639} (2024).  Open; the paper prints $q<p$, which appears to be a contextual typo for $q<m$.}
{Perform the full Lyapunov--Schmidt reduction, calculate the interaction matrix, and read the components from the resulting finite-dimensional Morse complex.}
{$\psettle=60\%$; lean true; wording confidence medium.}

\cand{Strong unique continuation on stationary varifolds}
{If $V$ is a stationary $m$-varifold and an ambient $C^1$ function $h$ is harmonic on $V$, then infinite-order $L^2(\|V\|)$ vanishing at $x_0$ forces $h$ to vanish on $\operatorname{spt}V$ near $x_0$.}
{C. Brena, S. Decio and C. De Lellis, Conjecture 1.7 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00651}{arXiv:2503.00651} (2025).  Open.}
{First establish a frequency inequality on two-plane and branched models; then stratify.  Conversely, glue an infinite-order branch to seek a counterexample.}
{$\psettle=50\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Normal balance of minimal hypersurfaces in a sphere}
{Every closed oriented, non-totally-geodesic minimal hypersurface $M^n\subset S^{n+1}$ satisfies $\int_M\nu\,d\mu=0$.}
{J. Ge and F. Li, Remark 2.9 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.07747}{arXiv:2103.07747} (2021).  No resolution was found.}
{Combine $\Delta\langle\nu,a\rangle=-|A|^2\langle\nu,a\rangle$ with Minkowski identities; in parallel, compute asymmetric cohomogeneity-one examples to test the unweighted integral.}
{$\psettle=70\%$; lean false.}

\cand{Stability of Type-II translator-tip singularities}
{Rotationally symmetric MCF data in the ``near class'' of Garfinkle--Isenberg--Knopf--Wu have the same finite-time Type-II singular behavior as the unperturbed IW/IWZ solution, including its maximum-curvature rate and rescaled tip limit.}
{Conjecture 4.1 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06613}{arXiv:2105.06613} (2021).  Supported numerically; open.}
{Prove a weighted spectral gap for the linearized translator, introduce modulation parameters, and close matched inner/outer barriers.}
{$\psettle=60\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Critical degenerate neckpinch asymptotics}
{A rotationally symmetric solution in the paper's critical class develops a local Type-II Angenent--Vel\'azquez neckpinch with integer $m\ge3$, tip scale $(T-t)^{-(1-1/m)}$, and a Type-I cylindrical neck.}
{Conjecture 5.1 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06613}{arXiv:2105.06613}.  Open.}
{Use one-parameter shooting to hit the codimension-one critical manifold, then control the neutral mode with center-manifold estimates.}
{$\psettle=55\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Finite-entropy translating graph classification}
{The plane and the bowl soliton are the only finite-entropy translating graphs whose planar domains are not contained in a strip.}
{E. Gama, F. Mart\'in and N. M\o ller, Introduction of \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.01640}{arXiv:2209.01640} (2022; revised 2025).  Open.}
{Combine end structure and Morse--Rad\'o theory with tilted grim-reaper and $\Delta$-wing barriers.}
{$\psettle=65\%$; lean true.}

\cand{No complete translator graph over a half-plane}
{The domain of every complete connected translating graph in $\mathbb R^3$ is either $\mathbb R^2$ or a strip; in particular, no half-plane occurs.}
{Remark 3.8 of \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.01640}{arXiv:2209.01640}.  Open.}
{Exploit Jenkins--Serrin flux on expanding half-disks and compare with tilted grim reapers to contradict completeness at the finite boundary line.}
{$\psettle=75\%$; lean true.}

\cand{No translating annulus between sufficiently close planes}
{There exists $\varepsilon>0$ such that no properly embedded connected translating annulus $M\subset\mathbb R^3$ is, outside a compact set, the disjoint union of two $C^2$ normal graphs converging at infinity to parallel vertical planes whose distance is less than $\varepsilon$.}
{This is the quantitative formulation adopted here from Remark 1.7, Figure 1, and the surrounding discussion of \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.11447}{arXiv:2304.11447} (2023).}
{Remove the bounded-slice assumption from their slab-width theorem using translator flux and Morse--Rad\'o control of vertical sections.}
{$\psettle=65\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Large-inner-width annuloids converge to the translating catenoid}
{For fixed neck size $s>0$, annuloids with inner widths tending to infinity converge, after vertical translation, to the rotational translating catenoid $W(s)$.}
{Conjecture 2.5 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.11447}{arXiv:2304.11447}.  Open.}
{Obtain uniform curvature and area bounds, take a translator limit, and use Alexandrov reflection plus annular uniqueness to identify it.}
{$\psettle=75\%$; lean true.}

\cand{A genus-one free-boundary minimal surface in every ellipsoid}
{Every ellipsoid in $\mathbb R^3$ contains an embedded $D_2$-equivariant free-boundary minimal surface of genus one with connected boundary.}
{A. Schulz, conjecture on p.~4 of \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13465}{arXiv:2406.13465} (2024).  Open.}
{Run a $D_2$-equivariant multiparameter min--max and exclude disk or annulus degeneration using a strict width inequality and equivariant topology.}
{$\psettle=75\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Critical spherical catenoid maximizes a Robin functional}
{Among annulus metrics, the critical spherical catenoid in $B^3(r)\subset S^3_+$ maximizes
$\Xi^+_{r,1}=\min\{\lambda_0(g,-\tan r),\lambda_1(g,\cot r)\}\operatorname{Area}_g$.}
{T. Ma and V. Medvedev, p.~5 of \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05160}{arXiv:2504.05160} (2025).  Open.}
{Use conformal uniformization of the annulus, Hersch balancing, and sharp Robin inequalities; characterize equality through the eigenfunction immersion.}
{$\psettle=65\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Higher-genus properness in Fine's asymptotic Plateau moduli problem}
{For a generic knot $K\subset S^3=\partial_\infty\mathbb H^4$, the fixed-topology and fixed-self-linking moduli space of properly immersed minimal fillings is compact for positive genus.}
{J. Fine, \S1.3 of \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.07713}{arXiv:2112.07713} (2021), restated in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26234}{arXiv:2605.26234} (2026).  The disk case is known; higher genus remains open.}
{Analyze and glue the explicit Hopf-link nodal model.  Either show the bad locus has codimension at least two or exhibit a generic nonproper family.}
{$\psettle=60\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Uniqueness of degenerate mean-convex ancient free-boundary MCF}
{For every unstable two-sided properly embedded free-boundary minimal hypersurface in an ambient manifold whose boundary is strictly convex ($\mathrm{II}>0$), the mean-convex ancient free-boundary flow converging to it in $C^{1,\alpha}$ is unique up to time translation and choice of side, without a sublinear decay assumption.}
{T. Bourni and G. Franz, future-directions discussion in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08850}{arXiv:2602.08850} (2026).  The nondegenerate case is proved; the degenerate case is open.}
{Combine order barriers with a Merle--Zaag spectral ODE bootstrap for the Jacobi kernel.}
{$\psettle=75\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Exact index of the Carlotto--Schulz hypertorus}
{For $n>2$, the Carlotto--Schulz minimal hypertorus has
$\operatorname{ind}(X^n_{CS})=(n^3+9n^2+11n+3)/3$, while $\operatorname{ind}(X^2_{CS})=27$.}
{O. Perdomo, Conjecture 1.1 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16980}{arXiv:2606.16980} (2026).  Verified numerically through a large range; open in general.}
{Separate into Sturm--Liouville/Floquet blocks and prove uniform Sturm comparison, using interval arithmetic only on the compact residual parameter range.}
{$\psettle=90\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Density purity implies stationary-varifold regularity}
{If a stationary integral varifold has a unique flat tangent cone at $x_0$ and the rescaled measure of points whose density differs from $\Theta(V,x_0)$ tends to zero, then $x_0$ is regular.}
{Brena--Decio--De Lellis, Conjecture 1.3 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00651}{arXiv:2503.00651} (2025).  Open.}
{Begin in codimension one or multiplicity two, combining excess decay with their $C^\infty$-rectifiability theorem; also test stationary analogues of Brakke constructions.}
{$\psettle=50\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Infinite-order minimal-graph approximant}
{Under the preceding density-purity hypotheses there is a smooth classical minimal graph $M$ through $x_0$ such that, for every $N$,
\[\int_{B_r(x_0)}\operatorname{dist}(x,M)^2\,d\|V\|(x)=o(r^N).\]}
{Brena--Decio--De Lellis, Conjecture 1.4 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00651}{arXiv:2503.00651}.  A sequel constructs a smooth approximating graph but does not prove it minimal.}
{Show that the approximating graph's formally flat mean-curvature jet is analytic or Gevrey; a nonanalytic flat germ is the natural counterexample mechanism.}
{$\psettle=55\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Infinite-order contact forces containment}
{If a stationary varifold has infinite-order $L^2$ contact at $x_0$ with a smooth classical minimal submanifold $M$, then its support is locally contained in $M$.}
{Brena--Decio--De Lellis, Conjecture 1.5 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00651}{arXiv:2503.00651}.  Open even when $M$ is a plane.}
{Develop a planar-varifold frequency monotonicity or Carleman inequality; in parallel, search branched stationary cones for an infinite-order attachment.}
{$\psettle=50\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Bounded mean curvature bounds slice index}
{For a smooth closed embedded Euclidean mean-curvature flow $M_t^n\subset\mathbb R^{n+1}$, $n\ge3$, up to its first singular time, a uniform bound on $|H|$ implies a uniform bound on the Morse index of $\Delta+|A|^2$ on each time slice.}
{Y. Han, Remark 1.3 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05489}{arXiv:2501.05489} (2025; revised 2026).  Open.}
{Localize negative modes and prove that each consumes a definite curvature, area, or entropy budget; alternatively glue many almost-minimal unstable regions while keeping $H$ bounded.}
{$\psettle=60\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Every critical hyperbolic catenoid has index four}
{For every parameter $a>1/2$, the critical hyperbolic catenoid $\Sigma_a\subset B^3(r(a))\subset\mathbb H^3$ has Morse index $4$.}
{V. Medvedev, conjecture after Theorem 1.4 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.02409}{arXiv:2311.02409} (2023; revised 2025).  A. Pigazzini, Corollary 11.10 of \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13562}{arXiv:2605.13562}, proves index four and nullity two for $a\in(1/2,1/2+\delta_0)$; the global statement is open.}
{Separate variables and reduce the residual range to a scalar monotonicity inequality, certified by interval arithmetic where analytic comparison is coarse.}
{$\psettle=85\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Hyperbolic energy-index comparison}
{Every $k$-dimensional free-boundary minimal $\Sigma\subset B(r)\subset\mathbb H^n$ satisfies $\operatorname{Ind}_E(\Sigma)\le n\operatorname{Ind}_S(\Sigma)$.}
{Medvedev, Remark 5.3 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.02409}{arXiv:2311.02409}.  This is the open hyperbolic analogue of a proved spherical estimate.}
{Replace the spherical componentwise comparison by Lorentz-signature splitting and gauge fixing; verify all rotational examples by ODE first.}
{$\psettle=60\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Peak boundary heat flux is maximized by a semidisk}
{For bounded convex planar $\Omega$, let $u_1$ be the $L^2$-normalized first Dirichlet eigenfunction.  The scale-invariant quantity $\|\partial_\nu u_1\|_{L^\infty(\partial\Omega)}/\lambda_1(\Omega)$ is maximized by a semidisk, at the midpoint of its diameter.}
{Wang--Hoskins--Rachh--Barnett, main conjecture in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16452}{arXiv:2603.16452} (2026).  The paper proves boundedness and semidisk stationarity; global optimality is open.}
{Compute the full shape Hessian, then combine a boundary-integral formulation with interval bounds and a global convexity/localization reduction.}
{$\psettle=60\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Existence and nondegeneracy of the optimal Steklov $N$-gon}
{Among convex planar polygons with at most $N$ sides and fixed perimeter, the supremum of $\sigma_1$ is attained by a polygon having exactly $N$ nondegenerate sides.}
{Conjecture 1.1 in \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2603.25116}{arXiv:2603.25116} (2026).  Open.}
{Use Blaschke compactness for existence and a strict corner-splitting shape derivative to rule out fewer or collapsed sides.}
{$\psettle=70\%$; lean true.}

\cand{The equilateral triangle maximizes the first Steklov eigenvalue}
{Among convex planar triangles of fixed perimeter, the equilateral triangle uniquely maximizes $\sigma_1$.}
{The $N=3$ case of Conjecture 1.2 in \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2603.25116}{arXiv:2603.25116} (2026).  It is open even in this first case.}
{Use conformal transplantation and certified two-parameter shape optimization, with corner asymptotics controlling the boundary of triangle moduli space.}
{$\psettle=55\%$; lean true.}

\cand{No convex polygon is Steklov-isospectral to a smooth simply connected domain}
{A convex planar polygon cannot have the same Steklov spectrum as a bounded simply connected planar domain with smooth boundary.}
{Dryden et al., Conjecture 6.17 in \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2604.18977}{arXiv:2604.18977} (2026).  Open.}
{Sharpen the heat or wave trace to isolate a corner-diffraction coefficient incompatible with the smooth expansion.}
{$\psettle=70\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Only finitely many infinite critical Steklov lengths}
{For every $n\ge2$ there is $K(n)$ such that, for every eigenvalue index $k\ge K(n)$, the normalized Steklov eigenvalue on a hypersurface of revolution with two unit $S^{n-1}$ boundary components attains its optimum at a finite meridian length.}
{Conjecture 8 of A. M\'etras and T. Tschanz, \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10743}{arXiv:2401.10743} (2024).  The case $n=2$ was previously known and the paper proves $n=3,4$; it is open for $n\ge5$.}
{Derive uniform Bessel/ODE asymptotics and prove eventual positivity of the branch derivative, leaving finitely many modes for certified checking.}
{$\psettle=75\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Magnetic second-eigenvalue phase transition}
{For area-one planar domains with constant magnetic field $B$, the second Dirichlet magnetic eigenvalue is minimized by two equal disks for $0\le B\le4\pi$ and by one disk for $B\ge4\pi$, with the stated transition at $4\pi$.}
{Conjecture 4.1 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06533}{arXiv:2412.06533} (2024).  Open.}
{Prove a magnetic Wolf--Keller decomposition and combine the disk Landau-level crossing with quantitative Faber--Krahn stability; scrutinize a neighborhood of $4\pi$ for a counterexample.}
{$\psettle=55\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Strong-field magnetic third eigenvalue is minimized by a disk}
{For every $B\ge6\pi$, the area-one disk minimizes the third Dirichlet magnetic eigenvalue among planar domains.}
{The first case not contained in the preceding phase-transition problem of Conjecture 4.2 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06533}{arXiv:2412.06533}.  Open.}
{Use three-dimensional magnetic Wolf--Keller decompositions, strong-field asymptotics, and quantitative isoperimetry; the exact threshold is also amenable to validated numerics.}
{$\psettle=55\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Positive exterior-Robin threshold in the hyperbolic plane}
{There is a bounded nonconvex simply connected $\Omega\subset\mathbb H^2$ whose exterior Robin Laplacian has a discrete eigenvalue below $1/4$ for some positive boundary parameter; equivalently $\alpha_*(\Omega^{\rm ext})>0$ in the source's convention.}
{Remark 3.11 of \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2601.10280}{arXiv:2601.10280} (2026).  Open.}
{Join two annular trapping components by a thin channel and prove norm-resolvent convergence while preserving simple connectivity.}
{$\psettle=85\%$; lean true.}

\cand{A five-facet Dirichlet-minimizing polytope is simple}
{A minimizer of $\lambda_1$ among convex three-dimensional polytopes of fixed volume with exactly five facets is simple: exactly three facets meet at every vertex.}

{This is a strengthened Baire-generic version adopted here from Open Question 10.6 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.12528}{arXiv:2212.12528}.  The two-dimensional localization phenomenon is known; the higher-dimensional generic statement is open.}
{Microlocally diagonalize the Dirichlet-to-Neumann operator by boundary component and prove generic nonresonance of the component spectra.}
{$\psettle=50\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Planar Steklov spectra determine smooth domains}
{No two noncongruent smooth bounded connected planar domains are Steklov-isospectral.}
{This is the negative-answer formulation adopted here for Open Question 9.11 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.12528}{arXiv:2212.12528}.  The survey asks whether such pairs can exist; the problem is open.}
{On the proof side, combine heat and wave invariants with the conformal representation of the planar Dirichlet-to-Neumann map; on the disproof side, test transplantation and Sunada-type constructions subject to planar embeddability.}
{$\psettle=50\%$; proof and counterexample routes are balanced.}

\cand{Linear-size law for interior Steklov nodal sets}
{For a fixed smooth $(d+1)$-dimensional domain $\Omega$ and Steklov eigenpairs $(u_k,\sigma_k)$, there are constants $c,C>0$ such that
\[c\sigma_k\le\mathcal H^d(\{u_k=0\}\cap\Omega^\circ)\le C\sigma_k.\]}
{The interior half of Open Question 10.7 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.12528}{arXiv:2212.12528}.  The sharp linear upper bound is known for real-analytic data; for smooth data the best cited surface bound is $O(\sigma^{3/2})$, with polynomial bounds in higher dimensions.}
{First test whether smooth nonanalytic boundaries permit superlinear growth; otherwise combine doubling estimates with a Logunov-style induction adapted to harmonic extensions.}
{$\psettle=50\%$ because the literal smooth statement is counterexample-friendly.}

\cand{Strominger space forms with nonzero curvature are K\"ahler}
{A compact Hermitian manifold whose Strominger--Bismut connection has constant nonzero holomorphic sectional curvature is K\"ahler.}
{X. Chen and F. Zheng, Conjecture 2 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.07108}{arXiv:2111.07108} (2021).  The compact locally conformally K\"ahler case is now proved in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01893}{arXiv:2608.01893}; the general conjecture remains open.}
{Classify genuinely non-LCK solvmanifold and balanced candidates algebraically; a small-dimensional non-K\"ahler homogeneous example would disprove the statement, while the resulting torsion identities suggest a Bochner proof.}
{$\psettle=55\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Chern space forms with nonzero curvature are K\"ahler}
{A compact Hermitian manifold with constant nonzero Chern holomorphic sectional curvature is K\"ahler.}
{The Chern half of Conjecture 1 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.07108}{arXiv:2111.07108}.  The compact LCK case is proved in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24425}{arXiv:2606.24425}, and \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05598}{arXiv:2608.05598} adds a balanced-threefold nonpositive-curvature case; the general problem is open.}
{Turn the constant-curvature equation into torsion identities on remaining non-LCK solvmanifolds and balanced classes, then seek an integral Bochner identity that removes the structural assumptions.}
{$\psettle=50\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Rozansky--Witten sign conjecture in complex dimension six}
{For an irreducible hyperk\"ahler manifold of complex dimension six, $b_{\Theta\Theta_2}<0$ and $b_{\Theta_3}>0$.}
{The first unproved dimension of Conjecture 1 (equivalently Conjecture 4) in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.11617}{arXiv:2112.11617} (2021).  The signs hold for all currently known examples.}
{Identify a pointwise sum-of-squares expression for the curvature contraction; symbolic tensor reduction already gives strong evidence.}
{$\psettle=60\%$; lean true.}

\cand{The $4\times4$ Lu--Wenzel two-eigenvalue inequality}
{For $X\in M_4(\mathbb C)$ with Frobenius norm one and $T_X(Y)=[X^*,[X,Y]]$, the ordered eigenvalues satisfy $\lambda_1(T_X)+\lambda_3(T_X)\le3$.}
{The first open matrix size in Lu--Wenzel Conjecture 4.11, surveyed in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01085}{arXiv:2402.01085} (2024).  Normal, rank-one, and $n\le3$ cases are known.}
{Stratify by full unitary-congruence invariants or Schur type, not singular values alone, and finish the residual semialgebraic optimization with interval certification.}
{$\psettle=70\%$; lean true.}

\cand{The $4\times4$ fundamental Lu--Wenzel commutator inequality}
{For real matrices $B=B_1,B_2,\ldots,B_m\in M_4(\mathbb R)$ satisfying $\operatorname{tr}(B_\alpha B_\beta^T)=0$ whenever $\alpha\ne\beta$ and, for every $2\le\alpha,\beta\le m$,
\[\operatorname{tr}\bigl(B_\alpha[B,B_\beta]\bigr)=0,\]
one has
\[\sum_{\alpha=2}^m\|[B,B_\alpha]\|^2\le\left(\max_{\alpha\ge2}\|B_\alpha\|^2+\sum_{\alpha=2}^m\|B_\alpha\|^2\right)\|B\|^2.\]}
{The first open size in Conjecture 4.9 of \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01085}{arXiv:2402.01085}; sizes at most three are proved in its Theorem 4.18.  The inequality unifies several submanifold curvature inequalities.}
{Use invariant theory and semidefinite sum-of-squares certificates on $M_4(\mathbb R)$; a rational violating tuple would settle it negatively.}
{$\psettle=55\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Existence of a shrinking $5$-loop under curve shortening}
{There is a $5$-loop whose curve-shortening flow remains a $5$-loop and shrinks to the origin.}
{The first open case of the all-$n$ conjecture in \S5.2 of Angenent et al., \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.09876}{arXiv:2403.09876} (2024); the cases $n=3,4$ are proved.}
{Generalize the finite-parameter sign-balancing argument used for $n=3,4$, with validated numerics controlling the shooting map.}
{$\psettle=65\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Anisotropic asymptotic shape of shrinking $3$-loops}
{A shrinking $3$-loop has inner profile $u(t,x)\sim K U_{2}(t-T,x)$ and, after bounding-box rescaling, converges to the closed squeezed bow tie
\[\{(x,y):|x|\le1,\ y=\pm x^{2}\}\cup\{(\pm1,y):|y|\le1\},\]
with the precise scale laws stated in the source.}
{\S6.3 of \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.09876}{arXiv:2403.09876}.  Open beyond the previously understood figure-eight behavior.}
{Use self-similar variables, spectral decomposition in Hermite modes, and modulation to suppress all modes below $n-1$.}
{$\psettle=60\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Discrete positive mass for asymptotically flat graphs}
{Every asymptotically flat graph with nonnegative discrete scalar curvature---defined from sums of Lin--Lu--Yau/Ollivier edge curvatures---has nonnegative discrete ADM mass, with equality only for the standard grid.}
{Hua--M\"unch--Zhang, Conjecture 1.6 in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08334}{arXiv:2307.08334} (2023).  Grid-combinatorial and stronger Ricci-nonnegative cases are proved; the conjecture is open.}
{Enumerate finite perturbations as a linear or mixed-integer program to seek negative mass, then derive a discrete divergence identity suggested by the dual certificate.}
{$\psettle=55\%$; proof and counterexample routes are both concrete.}

\cand{Hexagonal Faber--Krahn conjecture}
{Among planar hexagons of prescribed area, the regular hexagon uniquely minimizes the first Dirichlet eigenvalue $\lambda_1$.}
{The $N=6$ case of the P\'olya--Szeg\H{o} polygonal conjecture; the modern finite-dimensional reduction is in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.16409}{arXiv:2203.16409}, with certified local methods developed in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11575}{arXiv:2406.11575}.  Global optimality remains open.}
{Combine the reduction theorem with interval-certified lower bounds on the compact complement of a local neighborhood of the regular hexagon.}
{$\psettle=80\%$; lean true.}

\cand{Hexagonal torsional-rigidity conjecture}
{Among planar hexagons of prescribed area, the regular hexagon uniquely maximizes the torsional rigidity $T(\Omega)=\int_\Omega u$, where $-\Delta u=1$ and $u|_{\partial\Omega}=0$.}
{The $N=6$ case of the P\'olya--Szeg\H{o} polygonal torsion conjecture; recent analytic and numerical evidence appears in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16450}{arXiv:2309.16450}, while \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2607.28768}{arXiv:2607.28768} proves only the tangential-polygon class.  The full case is open.}
{Prove a tangentialization monotonicity or reduce the remaining polygonal moduli space to finitely many interval-certified boxes.}
{$\psettle=75\%$; lean true.}

\end{enumerate}

\section{The explicit counterexample to L1}\label{app:xu}

Let $M=[-1,1]_y\times T^{n-1}$, $n\ge3$, with its flat product metric.  Its scalar curvature and boundary mean curvature vanish.  With
\[a=\frac{4(n-1)}{n-2},\qquad p=\frac{2n}{n-2},\qquad u=A-y^2\quad(A>1),\]
the conformal Laplacian with Xu's Robin condition is $-a\Delta$ with Neumann boundary condition.  Constants show $\eta'_1=0$, and integration by parts shows it is the first eigenvalue.  The conformal metric $\widetilde g=u^{p-2}g$ satisfies
\[S_{\widetilde g}=u^{1-p}(-a\Delta u)=2a u^{1-p}>0.\]
At both boundary components the outward derivative is $\partial_\nu u=-2$ and $u=A-1$, hence Xu's transformation formula gives
\[H_{\widetilde g}=\frac{p-2}{2}u^{-p/2}\partial_\nu u
=-(p-2)(A-1)^{-p/2}<0.\]
Thus $H$ is nonzero while $S$ is everywhere positive and
\[\int_M S\,dV_g=2a\operatorname{Vol}(T^{n-1})\int_{-1}^{1}(A-y^2)^{1-p}\,dy>0.\]
Both proposed necessary conditions fail.  If one wants connected boundary and orientability, quotient by the free orientation-preserving isometry
$(y,x_1,x_2,\ldots)\mapsto(-y,-x_1,x_2+1/2,\ldots)$; $u$ descends and the computation is unchanged.

\section{Dependence and triage warnings}

The one hundred statements are not one hundred independent Bernoulli trials.  In particular, L6--L7, L8--L10, L11--L13, L15 and L28--L30, L17--L18, M1--M2, M9--M13, M15--M20, M22--M23, M31--M36, M46--M47, M26 with M49, and M27 with M50 share substantial machinery.  A successful project should start with a falsification sprint: reproduce definitions and conventions, search the simplest symmetry reductions, run low-dimensional symbolic or interval computations, and only then invest in a global proof.

The most attractive first attacks, on the present evidence, are L1, L8, L27, L32, L40, L49, M12, M20, M26, M44, and M49.  They combine a sharp literal statement with either an explicit ODE/matrix reduction, a finite certified computation, or a conspicuous counterexample mechanism.
